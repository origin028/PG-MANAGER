================================================================================
SMART GROUP PG MANAGEMENT SYSTEM — CLIENT INSTALLATION & OPERATIONS GUIDE
================================================================================

Welcome to the Smart Group PG Management System! This guide outlines the steps
to install, configure, and operate the Admin Web, Admin Android App, and Resident App.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. ADMIN WEB PORTAL (Browser Access)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- URL: Accessible via any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
- Recommended: Bookmark the URL on desktop/tablet for daily property management.
- Initial Login:
    1. Navigate to the Admin Web URL.
    2. Enter your registered Administrator Email and Password.
    3. Click "Sign In" to access the real-time PG Management Dashboard.
    4. If registering for the first time, click "Register as PG Owner" and enter
       the authorized Company Super Key provided during onboarding.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ADMIN ANDROID APPLICATION (APK Installation)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- File Location: release/admin-app.apk
- Installation Steps:
    1. Transfer 'admin-app.apk' to your Android smartphone or tablet.
    2. Open the file on your device (via File Manager or Downloads).
    3. If prompted with "Install unknown apps", tap "Settings" and toggle
       "Allow from this source".
    4. Tap "Install" and wait for completion.
    5. Tap "Open" to launch PG Management Admin.
    6. Sign in with your Administrator credentials.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. RESIDENT ANDROID APPLICATION (APK Installation)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- File Location: release/resident-app.apk
- Target Audience: PG Residents and Tenants.
- Installation Steps:
    1. Share 'resident-app.apk' with residents via WhatsApp, Email, or Drive.
    2. Residents install the APK by tapping "Install".
    3. Upon launch, residents enter their registered Mobile Number or Email.
    4. Tap "Get OTP" to receive a 6-digit verification code.
    5. Enter the OTP to instantly access:
        - Monthly rent amount and due dates.
        - Room and floor details.
        - Direct 1-tap UPI payments (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM).
        - Property food menus and daily meals.
        - Maintenance and service ticket requests.
        - Notice board announcements.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. INITIAL SETUP CHECKLIST FOR PROPERTY OWNERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Properties -> Add your PG Building details (Name, Address, Total Floors).
Step 2: Rooms & Beds -> Define rooms per floor, bed sharing, and monthly rent rates.
Step 3: Residents -> Add residents with their contact info and allocate beds.
Step 4: Invoices & Rent -> Generate monthly rent invoices or create test payments.
Step 5: Staff -> Register housekeeping and maintenance staff.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
